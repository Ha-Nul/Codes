\begin{abstract}
Studies about quantum thermodynamics in the information-theoretic aspects bring out how Information as its quantum nature, 
consumed as correlation, can change the direction of energy flow from hot to cold, which is called anomalous energy flow(AEF). 
Here, we study the bounds of AEF in the system-catalyst model by adding the symmetrical constraint for the quantum map, 
which can reinforce the previous result by adding the dynamical point of view. 
Our result shows that by constraining the symmetric property of spending information, 
the maximal bound of AEF increases. We show our result in the coherent catalyst case, 
which, depending on the Caldeira-Leggett model, can be reproduced using the quantum optics method.
\end{abstract}
